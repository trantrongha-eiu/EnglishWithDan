1 You can use this machine to make a hole in something. For example, you use a small one to make a hole in the wall to hang a picture, or a big one to make a hole in the ground.
2 This is a hole in the ground which is dug in order to extract water, oil or gas.
3 This is a kind of rock deep in the ground that isn't very hard and is easy to break.
4 This is a narrow space which appears inside rock when the rock breaks. The space can fill up with water or gas.
