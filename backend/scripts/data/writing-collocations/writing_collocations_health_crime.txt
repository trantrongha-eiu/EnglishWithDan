@word
word=excessive consumption of
meaning=việc tiêu thụ quá mức
example=Excessive consumption of sugary drinks has been linked to a sharp rise in childhood obesity.
definition=The act of eating or drinking far more of something than is healthy or necessary.
collocations=overconsumption of|excessive intake of|overindulge in
distractors=moderate use of|complete avoidance of|occasional enjoyment of

collocations=inactive lifestyle|lack of physical activity|desk-bound routine
distractors=active lifestyle|balanced routine|outdoor lifestyle

@word
word=junk food
meaning=đồ ăn vặt / thức ăn có hại

collocations=fast food|processed food|pre-made food
distractors=organic food|home-cooked meal|nutritious food

@word
word=have a balanced diet

collocations=have a healthy diet|maintain a nutritious diet|eat a well-balanced diet
distractors=have an imbalanced diet|follow a strict diet|skip meals regularly

collocations=have an unhealthy diet|eat poorly|follow a poor diet
distractors=have a balanced diet|eat in moderation|follow a strict regime
